# Qutrit Clifford+D and Clifford+R have the same asymptotic compilation slope

*Version 2026-06-04. Author: Henry Lamm (Fermilab/SQMS). "Nick" refers to Nick Gnedin (Fermilab/SQMS), the project's parallel exact-ring C+D branch.*

**TL;DR.** For generic $U(3)$ targets, qutrit Clifford+D (over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9, 1/3]$) and qutrit Clifford+R (over $\mathbb{Z}[\omega_3, 1/3]$) approximate-synthesize a target $T$ within a **narrow $[4.1, 6.2] \log_3(1/\varepsilon)$ band straddling the $SU(3)$ information-theoretic floor** $N_G = 4.9 \log_3(1/\varepsilon) - 2.16$ (Gustafson `paper_main.tex` line 654). Specific algorithm choice moves the constant inside this band; crucially the extra algebraic richness of $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9]$ does **not** move it outside the band in the favorable direction. The extra Galois places buy richer **exact** synthesis but no improvement in the **approximation** slope.

## The result

The synthesis target $T$ (here a $R_z(\theta)$ rotation on a 1-D submanifold of $SU(3)$) is approximated by a ring element $V$ through the principal complex embedding $\sigma_1$ only; the physical channel never reads the other Galois conjugates. The key identity is the trace decomposition $3\, q(x) = |\sigma_1(x)|^2 + |\sigma_2(x)|^2 + |\sigma_4(x)|^2$ for $x \in \mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9, 1/3]$, with the Kalra $q$-form bounded by $q(x) \le B = 2 \cdot 3^{2f}$ at sde-depth $f$. The Path-C partial theorem proves the $\sigma_1$-projection covering radius is $\Omega(B^{-1/2})$ (Minkowski second), matched by the trivial average-spacing $O(B^{-1/2})$ — hence $\Theta(B^{-1/2})$, the same asymptotic rate as the smaller $\mathbb{Z}[\omega_3, 1/3]$ lattice. (Caveat: this is a rate-matching theorem; it does **not** prove the constants in the resulting $c \cdot \log_3(1/\varepsilon)$ slope are identical between the two rings — the slope agreement at the *constant* level is empirical, not theorem-derived.) The extra four real dimensions $(\sigma_2, \sigma_4)$ are spent on $q$-budget, not on covering. Demanding cover $< \varepsilon$ forces $f \gtrsim \log_3(1/\varepsilon)$, and each word costs $O(f)$ non-Clifford gates on both rings.

The empirics line up. Bocharov's 6-D meniscus polytope factors **exactly** as (2-D meniscus in $\sigma_1$) $\times$ (4-D $q$-ball in $\sigma_2, \sigma_4$); an analytically derived count of $\sim 3 \times 10^{29}$ points at $f{=}12$, $\varepsilon{=}10^{-4}$ (Minkowski-volume integration over the factored polytope, cross-checked against direct enumeration at $f{=}1, 2$) gives $\theta$-independent confirmation that the extra places are a free 4-D ball (`bocharov_z9_port_dead`). Per-cluster $N_D$ from Nick's exact-ring C+D vs Gustafson's C+R Householder fit $N^H_R = 3.20 + 5.139 \log_3(1/\varepsilon)$ (`paper_main.tex` eq:numhos lines 631–633) agrees within $\sim 2$ gates across $f{=}4{\ldots}14$ (at $f{=}8$ 62.6 vs 62.9; at $f{=}12$ 90.2 vs 91.1; at $f{=}14$ 102.7 vs 105.0); at $f{=}16$ the Nick mean rises to 119.7 vs Householder prediction 116.2, and the slope refit over $f{=}4{\ldots}16$ rises to $5.118$ — matching Householder rather than sitting between the two C+R variants. Nick's fit restricted to $f{=}4{\ldots}14$ is $N_D = 5.97 + 4.890 \log_3(1/\varepsilon)$ ($R^2 = 0.896$, $n{=}750$; slope robust under jackknife $\sim 4.89 \pm 0.2$ per `cd_vs_cr_corrected`), formally sitting between C+R Exhaustive ($4.113 \log_3$, eq:numexh) and Householder ($5.139 \log_3$); over the full $f{=}4{\ldots}16$ window ($n{=}900$) the Nick slope is $5.118$ and statistically indistinguishable from Householder ($z = -0.34$). Both bracket the gate-set-independent $SU(3)$ information-theoretic floor $4.90 \log_3(1/\varepsilon) - 2.16$ (the C+R Exhaustive fit $4.11$ dips below the asymptote because the bound is an $\Omega$-bound with a finite-$\varepsilon$ offset, not an equality — see Gustafson `paper_main.tex` line 654). Independently, Babai-CVP on $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9]$ in its single-vector Phase 3 form (independent $(x_1, x_3)$ picks) — the most natural geometric attempt to **exploit** the extra Galois dimensions — is empirically $2.6$–$9\times$ **worse** than HRSA at $\varepsilon \in [10^{-2}, 10^{-3}]$ and ${\sim}0\%$ success at $\varepsilon \le 10^{-4}$ (`babai_cvp_phase5_negative`); the JOINT Selinger §7 enumeration variant remains unfalsified but is expected to improve constants only.

## Why nobody noticed before

- **No working C+D approximate-synthesis algorithm existed pre-2026.** Kalra 2023 and Deaconu-Kalra-Mosca-Yard 2025 are exact-synthesis only; Evra-Parzanchevski 2024 is a non-constructive arithmeticity proof. The Lamm-group HRSA + zeta9 + Nick pipelines are the first to produce empirical $N_D$ vs $\log_3(1/\varepsilon)$ curves at all.
- **Community expectation ran the opposite way.** Glaudell-Ross-vdW-Yeh 2022 (arXiv:2202.09235) proved $C+R \subsetneq C+T$ (with $R$ requiring two qutrits to express in C+T). Combined with $T \in C+D$ (since $\omega = \xi^3$ for $\xi = e^{2\pi i / 9}$), the chain $C+R \subsetneq C+T \subseteq C+D$ made it natural to expect C+D would strictly out-perform C+R, not match it.
- **EvPar Theorem 4.14** gives a non-constructive $c{=}1$ bound $K > \log_3(105) \approx 4.236$ on $\log_\rho(1/\varepsilon)$ with $\rho = \sqrt{105}$; normalized per $\log_3(1/\varepsilon)$ this is a slope of $\sim 2$, but the absence of a constructive rounder means it sets neither a lower bound on what synthesis achieves nor a target anybody had reached. There was no calibrated comparison point.
- **The $\sigma_1$-projection / Path-C framing is novel for qutrits.** It is a Selinger / Bourgain-Gamburd-style lattice-density argument applied specifically to the 6-D vs 4-D embedding gap; nobody had executed that computation in the qutrit setting.

## What is still on the table for C+D

The slope-equivalence is asymptotic and target-generic; **finite-$\varepsilon$ wins for C+D remain real**. (i) **Ring-native exact synthesis**: targets exactly in $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9, 1/3]$ but not $\mathbb{Z}[\omega_3, 1/3]$ (specifically ninth-root phases $\xi^k = e^{2\pi i k/9}$) are zero-cost in C+D and $O(\log_3(1/\varepsilon))$ in C+R — Nick's exact branch already realizes this. (Note: the standard qutrit QFT uses $\omega_3$, not $\zeta_9$, so it is **not** an example here.) (ii) **Additive prefix savings**: smaller $K$ in $K + s \log_3(1/\varepsilon)$ where exact elements are denser near generic targets; helps at $\varepsilon \gtrsim 10^{-3}$. (iii) **Algorithmic upgrade Householder $\to$ Exhaustive within C+D** could *speculatively* close the $\sim 19\%$ gap to C+R Exhaustive ($4.11 \log_3$); Path-C is rate-matching across all algorithms on the same ring, so C+D Exhaustive could equally well land *below* $4.11$ or near Householder — direction is empirically unknown. ESA tabled at 45 GB lookup at $f{=}4$, so this needs algorithmic shrinkage before it can be tested. (iv) **Closed-form short words for structured target subclasses** — e.g. nontrivial $\xi^k$-phase compositions exploiting the full Galois symmetry — where the $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9]$ lattice's extra automorphisms could yield shorter ring expressions than $\mathbb{Z}[\omega_3]$. (Speculative; no specific construction in hand.) None of these change the slope. **Practical headline for hardware audiences**: even at the slope-equivalence operating point, C+D Nick beats two-qubit C+T emulation by $1.61\times$ asymptotically and $1.37\times$ at $\varepsilon = 10^{-10}$ (`cd_vs_cr_corrected_2026-05-29`).

## Strategic implications

For the paper: **frame C+D as "matches C+R on the asymptotic slope, with C+D's structural advantages cashed in at the exact and finite-$\varepsilon$ level"**, not "C+D beats C+R." The retracted $1.67\times$ headline (a log-base artifact) stays retracted; the honest framing is the convergence of independent backends — HRSA, zeta9, Nick on C+D; Exhaustive, Householder on C+R — onto a tight $[4.1, 6.2] \log_3(1/\varepsilon)$ band straddling the $SU(3)$ info-floor $4.90 \log_3(1/\varepsilon) - 2.16$. **Deprioritize**: further single-vector Babai-CVP variants (Phase 5 negative), Bocharov-on-$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9]$ ports (free-ball killed it), any project predicated on a C+D slope below C+R Exhaustive. **Worth scoping**: a true Exhaustive-class C+D backend (closes the constant gap), Selinger §7 joint $(x_1, x_3)$ enumeration (last unfalsified Babai variant — expected to improve constants, not slope), scaffolded-SK to push past zeta9's $\varepsilon{=}10^{-5}$ ceiling toward the paper's $\varepsilon{=}10^{-10}$ deliverable (scaffolded SK currently reaches $\varepsilon \approx 2 \times 10^{-10}$ at depth=5 with the tightened commutator polish; full end-to-end $\varepsilon{=}10^{-10}$ with canonical-form $N_D$ is in progress per `sk_commutator_tightened_2026-05-24`). A qutrit-KMM analog and RUS protocols would help both rings symmetrically and are out of scope here.
